Production isometric environment SPRITE ATLAS for EMBERGRAVE dark fantasy RPG. Strict grid of equal rectangular cells; each object isolated and centered inside its cell with generous transparent margins, no overlap. Painted realistic game sprites, aged ivory limestone, charcoal recesses, tarnished gold, restrained violet-blue light, upper-left lighting. Orthographic isometric down 30 degrees, front/right faces visible, 2:1 ground edges. TRUE TRANSPARENT RGBA background over entire sheet and between objects. No labels, text, checkerboard, scenery backdrop, characters or UI. 3 columns x 2 rows, SIX cells. Row1 left tall ruined wall with enormous fractured blue stained-glass rose window; center ruined Cinderwatch stone-and-charred-timber house facade with warm abandoned hearth; right a Last Bastion battlement fragment with blue banners and war damage. Row2 left snowless mountain stone shrine with jagged natural rock surrounding an ivory altar; center Malthoron's empty black stone throne on a small gold-inlaid dais, towering broken halo behind; right a ruined village memorial made of a cracked obelisk and low stone basin. No full buildings or roofs covering walkable rooms, just landmark cutouts. Landscape 1536x1024.
