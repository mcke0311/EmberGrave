How to read these numbers

Item percentages are the chance of at least one copy per normal monster, elite, boss, chest, or breakable. Gear accounts for all attempts and the source level −1 / 0 / +1 rolls. Base equipment rows combine common, enhanced, and rare outcomes; named uniques and sets have separate rows. Magic Find uses the game's diminishing returns for uniques and adjusted rarity weights.

Where to find lists standard zone spawn definitions at the selected difficulty, plus container odds where a container exists. It does not measure spawn frequency, clear speed, or container density. Scripted encounters, event caches, rich chests (+40 Magic Find), shops, crafting, and focused glyph rewards can use different rules. Boss-owned reinforcements do not drop loot. Quest rewards listed in item details are one-time chances.
