# Embergrave: get the first 25 players

Prepared September 14, 2026. This is a suggested first-week target, not a traffic forecast. Start with people who enjoy classic loot-driven action RPGs and can give feedback on a short browser session.

**Lead with:** Play a dark-fantasy action RPG free in your browser. Five classes, randomized loot, and a five-act world.

**Send everyone to:** [embergravegame.com](https://embergravegame.com/).

## Do this first

1. Publish the 40-second trailer on your main social account with the short post in [POSTS.md](POSTS.md). Put the playable link in the post itself.
2. Use the longer playtest post in one community that welcomes it. Ask people to try the opening for ten minutes and tell you where they became confused.
3. Reply to each substantive response. Record problems and follow up when you fix them.

The opening through Frosthaven gives new players a specific, manageable trial. Lead with solo play; introduce the four-player co-op beta in a later post.

## A manageable first week

| Day | Action | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Publish one short trailer post. | Post link, responses, reported plays. |
| 2 | Give useful feedback on other games; submit one eligible playtest request. | Where players get stuck in the opening. |
| 3 | Answer comments and reproduce the most common issue. | Device, browser, class, and exact problem. |
| 4 | Share one class screenshot with a short explanation of its play style. | Which class people want to try. |
| 5 | Invite interested players to a small co-op beta session. | Whether joining and playing together works for them. |
| 6 | Share a development update explaining a fix prompted by feedback. | Responses from the original reporters. |
| 7 | Review the response log and choose the next improvement. | Confirmed players, useful reports, and people who returned. |

Aim for 25 confirmed players, 10 useful reports, and 5 people who choose to return. Those are working targets. Do not count video views as players. The game entrypoint reviewed here has no obvious analytics integration; use reported plays and replies until measurement is deliberately added.
