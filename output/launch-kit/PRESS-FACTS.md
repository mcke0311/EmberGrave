# Embergrave: The Sunderstone Saga

**Play:** https://embergravegame.com/

**Support development:** https://ko-fi.com/embergrave

**Genre:** Dark-fantasy, isometric action RPG.

**Platform:** Web browser. Mouse and keyboard on desktop; landscape touch controls on phones.

**Access:** Free browser play with optional Ko-fi support.

**Status:** Ongoing development; multiplayer is a four-player co-op beta.

## Description

Embergrave: The Sunderstone Saga is a dark-fantasy action RPG built around class skills, randomized loot, and a five-act campaign. The journey begins on the snowy road to Frosthaven and continues through marshes, buried ruins, a shattered cathedral, and the Throne of Cinders. Choose a Vanguard, Ember Witch, Gravebinder, Veil Ranger, or Wildkeeper, build your abilities, and face bosses whose attacks change through the fight.

## Features

- Five playable classes with distinct skills and perk choices.
- Five campaign acts with different regions, hubs, quests, and bosses.
- Randomized equipment, socketed gear, crafting, and storage.
- Solo play and a separate four-player co-op beta.
- Browser play with desktop and landscape phone controls.

## Player notes

Saves are stored locally in the browser and are specific to the website address. Solo and co-op use separate heroes. Physical-device coverage varies; report the device and browser when describing a problem. The multiplayer service can take time to wake after inactivity.
