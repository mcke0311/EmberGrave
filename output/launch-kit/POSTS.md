## Playtest community post

**Title:** Embergrave — a free browser action RPG; looking for feedback on the first ten minutes

**Game Title:** Embergrave: The Sunderstone Saga

**Playable Link:** https://embergravegame.com/

**Platform:** Web browser. Desktop controls and landscape phone controls.

**Free to Play Status:** Free to play; optional support through Ko-fi.

**Involvement:** I'm the creator of Embergrave and I'm looking for feedback to guide its development.

**Description:**

Embergrave is a dark-fantasy, isometric action RPG built around fighting monsters, finding gear, and developing your class. You can play it in your browser without installing a game client.

Choose a Vanguard, Ember Witch, Gravebinder, Veil Ranger, or Wildkeeper. The solo campaign begins on the snowy road to Frosthaven, where you rescue travelers and fight your way toward the town. Beyond that opening are five acts with different regions, quests, and bosses, plus randomized loot and skill choices to experiment with.

I'm especially interested in how the first ten minutes feel to someone who hasn't seen the project before. Please try Single Player, choose whichever class interests you, and tell me where movement, combat, or the next objective becomes unclear. Mention your device and browser if you run into a problem.

There is also a four-player co-op beta, which uses separate heroes from solo play.

**Feedback I'm looking for:**

- Where did you first feel confused or stuck?
- Did movement and attacking feel responsive?
- What would make you want to play another session?

Thanks for trying it. I'll read the replies and use the feedback to improve the opening.

The Sunderstone is broken. Your journey begins on the frozen road to Frosthaven.

Embergrave is a dark-fantasy action RPG about finding your next weapon, shaping your character, and surviving what waits beyond the town walls.

Choose your path:

- **Vanguard:** close combat, warcries, charges, and slams.
- **Ember Witch:** fire, frost, and lightning.
- **Gravebinder:** raise the fallen, spread curses, and detonate corpses.
- **Veil Ranger:** ranged attacks, traps, and shadow skills.
- **Wildkeeper:** animal companions, storms, and beast transformations.

Travel through five campaign acts, from the Fallen North to the Throne of Cinders. Find randomized loot, socket equipment, choose skill perks, and face bosses with changing attack patterns.

Play solo or try the four-player co-op beta. Desktop has mouse and keyboard controls; phones use landscape touch controls. Saves are stored in your browser, so return using the same browser and website address.

Development is ongoing. Feedback on combat, controls, and the opening journey is welcome.
